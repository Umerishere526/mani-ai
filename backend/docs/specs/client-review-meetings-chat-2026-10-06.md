WRONG RESPONSE:
"Being left off those invites" sounds written rather than conversational.

"brings up real questions about where you stand" is even worse. It is dramatic, vague, and unnecessarily escalates the situation.

The user said she is wondering whether her role is changing. MANI immediately introduces "where you stand," which makes the situation sound more threatening than the evidence supports.

This is the first indication that Direct is being interpreted as stiff and blunt instead of clear, warm, and human.

WHAT IS WRONG:
The underlying question is reasonable, but "Did something shift recently?" is vague. Shift what?

And "did this start without any notice?" is awkward wording.

The whole response sounds composed rather than spoken.

WHAT MANI SHOULD HAVE SAID:
"Did this start recently, or have you been left out of meetings for a while?"

And again, the sentence does not sound like normal conversation.
