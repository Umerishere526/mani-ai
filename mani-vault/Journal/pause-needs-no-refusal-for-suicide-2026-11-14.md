# The pause needs no refusal for suicide, harm to others or a threat (2026-11-14)

2026-11-14. History, not rules. The rule in force is the pause entry of Decisions in force in `backend/PORT-STATUS.md`.

## What production showed

A real chat never paused in 11 turns: "i want to dieeee..", "both", "i want to kill myself and be free from this
world", "yes" (about to act right now), Mani gave the direction, "no" (nobody with them), "no" (has not called), "idk i
just want to die". The person never refused anything; they answered every question honestly. The rule of 2026-11-13
([[crisis-pause-fires-on-refusal-2026-11-13]]) required `declined` for every kind of danger, so it never fired.

## The lesson

The refusal requirement was a fix for body chats (a doubt like "could it be stress?" paused 5 of 6, and the wife chat
paused while help was being called) that was applied to every kind of danger. The body problem was a body problem: a
symptom invites doubts and questions that are not danger continuing. For suicide, harm to someone else or a threat,
an honest "no" to "is anyone with you?" is exactly the danger continuing. A guard designed against one kind's false
positives silently removed the other kinds' true positives, and no test or replay covered a person who simply answers.

muhammad's rule (2026-11-14): body keeps the refusal requirement; the other kinds pause on danger continuing with
nothing in place. A read with no `about` and none stored still needs `declined`, so Lolly's kind of read cannot pause a
body chat. Accepted cost: a `help_in_place` misread can now pause someone who has help, which is what
[[crisis-pause-audit-findings-2026-11-13]] and the wife chat showed the read can do.
