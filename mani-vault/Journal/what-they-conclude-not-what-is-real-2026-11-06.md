# What they conclude, not what is real (2026-11-06)

The parents chat understood the hurt well and then aimed ABCDE at a fact. "They don't trust my decisions" was an
accurate reading of years of dismissals, so D asked for a time they listened, found none, asked "do you have enough
information?", and got a yes that confirmed the belief. The belief keeping her stuck was a different one: "they will
never let me do what I want", which is why she stays quiet.

So the core under a real situation is what the person concludes from it, and D on a real pattern asks whether that
conclusion helps them. It never asks them to doubt what they have lived.

The close had the opposite problem: it carried the step forward but connected it to nothing. It now names what hurt,
the step, and how the step might help with one thing from the stages, as a possibility. It never promises another
person will change, and it is honest when the step can't give what they want, such as someone else in place of a
parent's support.

C ending on "nothing" was not a bug: a stage known from the chat is confirmed once, and agreement moves it. The swap
makes that confirmation ask a part not yet explored instead of "anything else?".
