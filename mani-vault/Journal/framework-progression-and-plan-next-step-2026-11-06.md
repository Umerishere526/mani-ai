# Steps move on when answered; Plan Next Step after the questions (2026-11-06)

replies in her first chat. The fix judges a stage by whether its question is answered, not by how much was said, and
caps exploring at one reply in code (a second explore is read as agreed). Therapists summarise and move on rather than
ask a follow-up per detail; Padesky's guided discovery puts the work in the summary and the synthesising question.

her plan the conversation she wanted to have. Now the closing reply says what is still ahead (conversation, action,
decision, none); Plan Next Step is offered only then, and the chat afterwards prepares it, for a conversation the way

and practising it with Mani as the other person.
