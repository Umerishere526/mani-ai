# Mani now talks like the reference chat (2026-10-13)

After Lolly's ChatGPT conversation, muhammad moved Mani toward it. Questions before an offer find the core of the concern, may give two or three possibilities taken from the person's words, and may say an understanding for them to confirm. The offer opens with two or three sentences naming the pattern across the conversation, then the client's pattern line. After Keep chatting, no offer for that reply and the two after, and Mani comes at it from another angle. "Just tell me what to do" gets: cannot hand over a solution, can help work it out, then the question that leads there. Structured Problem-Solving stays paused. A question is skipped only when any question would be unreasonable.

## Rules reversed, and why

- Menus of choices, summaries and "naming the pattern" had been banned on 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-10, because they led Mani to tell people what they feel. muhammad reversed it: the reference chat does these and the person recognises themselves in them. The guard that remains is that the understanding is stated as Mani's understanding, for them to confirm, never as fact.
- `heading_toward` no longer only decides the offer; questions may reach for what the set still needs, through their words.
- A measured finding the same day: with no floor, 12 of 36 runs on rich first messages were offered ABCDE in the first reply. Rule: never offer in the first reply; say back the core and ask what would show it is right.
