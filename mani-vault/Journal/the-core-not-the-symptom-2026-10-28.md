# The core, not the symptom (2026-10-28)

muhammad ran the weight-loss case in Directive. Every line of Mani's check came from her, and she said yes, yet the
core it named was wrong: "caught between wanting to move forward and protecting yourself from another
disappointment". That is what the core leads to. Her thoughts ran "I've ruined everything", "I lack discipline",
"other people manage, why can't I", "I'll never change", and they stop at "I'm not capable of change". The feeling
under her guilt is shame, and under the frustration, hopelessness. Mani listed "incapable" as a feeling.

Why it happened was ours, not the model's:

- The `heart` field and `conversation.md` asked for "what they are torn between, what they want, what is stopping
  them". That shape came from Lolly's boss and friendship chats, where there were two pulls. A weight-loss story has
  one harsh belief, so the model filled "what is stopping them" with the nearest thing, fear.
- "The feeling underneath." had been cut from the prompts in an earlier pass, and a test held the cut. Nothing asked
  Mani to look under the first feeling.
- The picture kept seven thoughts side by side with nothing saying which one the others lead to.
- A yes to a mostly true, warm reading is easy to give. It shows Mani was not wrong, not that it reached the core.

What changed: a `core` in the picture (belief, the feeling underneath as Mani's reading, about whom), the step
"what it says about them" and a section "Finding the core" in `conversation.md`, `heart` as exploring, core_named,
ready, and a check about the core alone (muhammad's choice). B and Thought Reframe's first stage start from the core.

To watch live: whether the model reports a core early and then deepens it, or settles on the first belief it sees;
whether naming the feeling underneath sounds like an invitation or like a diagnosis; and whether `reasoning_effort`
low is enough to find the layer, which was flagged as the next lever if reruns still miss.
