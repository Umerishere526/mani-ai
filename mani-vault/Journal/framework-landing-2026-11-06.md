# Framework Landing (2026-11-06)

muhammad's work-mistake chat reached a real shift at E ("being capable doesn't mean being perfect") and then simply
ended. The overchecking, the avoided responsibilities and the replaying after work, all named at C, were never touched.
His principle: "the framework ends when the person has reached a useful point, not simply when the framework's
questions have been answered."

A therapist ending a CBT exercise consolidates the discovery, checks it feels true, connects it to life outside the
session and agrees on a next step if one fits. That became a shared last stage, `landing`, merged into every framework
at seed like the somatic route: no letter, no announcement, Mani's own words carrying on from E. It uses the P1 stage
machinery (parts to explore, no cap, stalls guard) and needs no new model call.

E now does one job, the new view; the step moved to landing and is never required. Plan Next Step went away because
landing does it inside the framework. What they took away and what is still open are kept after the questions, so the
chat that follows never treats a finished framework as a solved problem.

Waiting on the client: their E asked for "one clear thing they'll focus on".
