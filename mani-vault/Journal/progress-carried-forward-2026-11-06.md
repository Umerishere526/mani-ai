# Progress carried between conversations (2026-11-06)

The family-guilt chat showed five gaps: Mani never asked what giving in did for them (the relief that keeps the
pattern alive), passed over "a bad son" as a feeling rather than a belief about who they are, gave insights that never
joined up, offered a step that was not aimed at the pattern, and would meet them next time as a stranger.

The first four are prompt and content changes. The fifth needed a new layer. muhammad's safeguards shaped it: keep the
person's words, keep discoveries apart from guesses, keep it current, and let them review, correct, delete and switch it
off. So progress is a small separate record (realised, trying, working on), bounded, folded with the existing memory
call rather than a new one, and never a copy of the conversation.
