# Every question earns its place (2026-11-06)

The same day's one-extra-turn cap was the wrong lesson from manasa's tests. muhammad: three questions at C are fine
when each opens a different part of her experience; what failed was asking without knowing what was already explored,
and giving one insight at most. "The goal is not to reduce the number of questions. The goal is to make every question
earn its place."

What changed is what Mani knows and is asked to do, not a count:

- **Reasoning before writing**, in the model's own hidden reasoning (GPT-6 Luna, medium): know, understand, infer,
  unknown, what helps most, where this turn goes. No visible checklist, nothing added to the wait.
- **A map of what is explored**, from the after-reply reading, now on framework turns too: areas explored, areas worth
  exploring next and why, connections Mani can infer but has not said. Per stage, the parts it can open and those it
  has (`covered`).
- **Progress per reply**: what each reply adds is kept, so the next adds something else, and "adds nothing" is counted.
- **Several aha moments**, tracked across chat and framework so none repeats; the first reply still never gives one.
- **Cadence on understanding**: the offer waits until the feeling, the belief or meaning, and the behaviour or need
  have been explored, on top of the floor and the confirmed core.
