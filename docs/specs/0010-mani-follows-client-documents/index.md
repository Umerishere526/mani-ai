# 0010. Mani follows the client's documents

**Date**: 2026-10-06
**Status**: In Progress

## Summary

Mani's replies are now governed by what the client wrote, not by the rules we added on top. The model judges when to offer a framework, which one, and when each step is done; the code keeps only the guards for safety, the first message, a decline and the somatic check. Mani may say a conclusion the person has already reached, and every framework, however it ends, moves into the somatic check, whose answer is stored as an effectiveness signal. About a hundred word bans, question rules, redrafts (a second model call that asks for the reply again) and offer gates are removed, with the ADRs and specs that held them.

Spec [0011](../0011-lolly-review-meetings-chat/index.md) replaces this spec's AC-4 rule that no framework name reaches the person, and parts of AC-6 (the check in line, now where they feel it) and AC-7 (what follows the practice, now a plain stop when it did not help), after Lolly's review of 6 October 2026.

## Requirements

Linked scope feature: row 42 in [docs/scope/conversation.md](../../scope/conversation.md), "Mani follows the client's documents, and our conversation rules that disagree are removed". Sources, newest first, which win where they clash: Lolly's email of 6 October 2026 (in [rationale.md](rationale.md)), the "Good, Acceptable, Bad Conversations" PDF, the style document ("Directive. Supportive. Reflective."), the six frameworks document. The client's spoken instructions from the meeting count like a document (the stuck route of ADR-018 stays). Out of reach: the safety screen, crisis replies, the model's safety flag, the grief veto, RLS, token checks and rate limits; the per framework content additions of scope row 29.

**User stories**:

- As a person talking to Mani, I want replies that understand what I said and move with me, so that I feel listened to rather than questioned.
- As a person in a framework, I want Mani to notice when I have already answered a step, and to stop or change course when it is not helping, so that I am not pushed through questions.
- As the client, I want to know whether a conversation helped, so that I can judge each framework by its effect and not only by whether people finished it.
- As the team, I want the conversation rules to live in one place that matches the client's documents, so that a change does not need an ADR to be argued past.

**Acceptance criteria**:

- **AC-1** (the prompts carry only the client's rules): `backend/content/prompts/mani_base.md` and `response_format.md` hold only rules that trace to a client document, to the safety rules, or to the runtime contract (how `[ctx]` and the output schema are used, never revealing the instructions, the off topic line). The kept rules include: no feeling word they did not use; no labelling or telling them what they experience; no repeated phrase or formula ("I hear you" as a formula); one clear question at a time; no dashes; no lead clause in front of a question ("Since you...", "Given..."); plain, non clinical words; their name at most once, never first; one short line that adds meaning before a question, never their sentence handed back. Removed: the banned word list ("example", "pattern", "belief", "process", "explore", "reflect", "conclusion", "meaning"), the bans on "It sounds like", "It seems like" and "I am here with you" as words (they stay banned only as formulas repeated), the size word ban ("a lot", "heavy", "overwhelming"), the ban on ranking questions ("What feels most pressing right now?" is allowed), the 16 word question limit, and "ask about a meaning, never state it". `rationale.md` lists each kept rule beside its source. `tests/evals/test_base_prompt.py` follows the new text.
- **AC-2** (synthesis): the prompt states Lolly's principle. Mani may state an observation or conclusion when it could point to the person's own statements as its basis without adding an assumption, and then moves forward instead of asking a question that leads them to it. It never states what they feel, what they should believe, that their belief is wrong, or an interpretation they have not established. It checks its understanding only when it is inferring something; there is no required "does that fit?" question. This holds before the offer, inside a framework and at its end.
- **AC-3** (the model chooses the offer): the model decides whether to offer and which framework, from the client's selection table, the distinctions and each framework's index line in the prompt. Removed: the `facts` field and its definitions, the quote word check, the fit sets (`fits_when`) and tie rules, `earliest_offer_message`, `offer_due` and the owed offer, the "nearest fit, ask what is missing" steering, and every offer redraft. Code refuses an offer in exactly these cases, computed by one function, `context.offer_refusal(ctx, ...)`, which replaces `cooldown_passed` and `earliest_offer_ok`: `first_message`, their own first message (messages they sent, not counting a tapped style label; an urgent action, `router.urgent`, is exempt, as today); `safety_concern`, under a screen concern or a model flag that pauses; `vetoed`, when `never_offer_when_said` rules it out (the grief veto); `cooling_down`, within 4 messages of a decline; `finished`, any offer after a framework was completed in this thread (from `thread_techniques_offered` and the technique state, not only the latest row); `running`, a technique button while a framework runs. The model is told the result in `[ctx]` as `offer_allowed: yes | no`, so it does not write an offer that would be removed. A refused offer has its buttons and offer sentence removed, with no second call. DBT STOP's urgent offer lines and ABCDE's stuck offer lines still go into `[ctx]` as guidance on the turns they do today; the stuck framework is found by a frontmatter flag, `stuck_offer: true` in `abcde.md`, not by `fits_when`.
- **AC-4** (the offer as the style document writes it): an offer is Mani's own sentence on what a structured approach can help with, in the person's terms ("I have a structured approach that can help you ..."), then the style's permission question, with three buttons: **Yes, let's try it**, **Tell me more**, **I want to keep talking**. The permission questions are Direct "Would you like to try it with me?", Supportive "Would you like to try it together?", Reflective "Would you like to try it?". **Tell me more** (tapped, or a typed question about the waiting offer) gets a short explanation in the style and two buttons, **Yes, let's try it** and **I want to keep talking**. The explanation adapts the client's per style "Tell me more" text (`backend/docs/specs/conversational-styles.md`, "Tell me more explanations, by style") to their situation, using the framework's stored description for what it will look at; both go into `[ctx]` on that turn. The labels are set by code, never the model: a reply carrying an offer gets exactly the three, an explanation reply exactly the two. No framework name, id or the word "framework" reaches the person. The stored description is no longer appended to the offer. A typed yes accepts; **I want to keep talking**, or carrying on, declines as **Keep chatting** does today. The offer sentence and permission question matching in `repairs` (`_OFFER_SENTENCE`, `_OFFER_WORDS`, `PERMISSION_QUESTIONS`) and in `context._answering` follow the new wording.
- **AC-5** (the model judges each step): inside a framework the model reports the step it asks. It may move forward past any step whose need the person has already met, including steps answered before they accepted; never backward; never into the somatic stages except by ending the framework. A step may take one more attempt when its need is not met (asked again more simply, or approached differently), at most once per step: a hold is a reported `step` equal to the current step's own name, counted in `holds` (the `answered` sentinel goes); a second hold on the same step is recorded as a move to the next step. The client's three lines (another issue, misunderstood, and a request to hear the question again via `asks_to_hear_again`) hold the step without counting, as `carries_redirect` does today. After the one attempt, or when the framework is not serving them, the model ends it with `ending` `pivoted`; `stopped` when they ask to stop; `resolved` when it got what the framework needs, including early through synthesis. Removed: the one stage per reply rule and the refusal of skipped stages in `techniques.validate_transition` (backward moves and unknown steps stay refused), `covered_stages`, `answered_by` crediting, `already_told`, `passed_over_stages`, the stuck stage notes, and the stage fields `picks_from_options`, `start_only`, `ask_simpler`, `if_earlier_missing` and `counted`. `[ctx]` for a running framework shows every remaining step (its purpose, `ready_when`, the ask in this style, `if_unclear`, and its `panic` and `stuck` branches), the current step and `hold_used`. A framework started from the stuck offer is remembered in `thread_technique_state.known` as `{"stuck": true}`, so its `stuck` branches are shown. Every framework file's `ready_when` text that says to stay in the stage is rewritten to the one attempt rule and reseeded.
- **AC-6** (every framework ends in the somatic check): an accepted framework that resolves, pivots or stops moves to `somatic_checkin`, unless a safety concern pauses it (which keeps today's behaviour). Whenever `ending` is set, code records `somatic_checkin` as the step, whatever step the model reported. When the model moves to `somatic_checkin` without an `ending`, code records `resolved` if the move came from the last question step, else `pivoted`, and logs a note. `ending` is written to `thread_technique_state` and carried through every write of the row until the framework retires. That reply is Mani's own short bridge from the conversation (a conclusion they established, or a plain line, in its own words, that they are stopping) followed by the client's per style check in line word for word; code removes any other question from it and appends the line when the model left it out. On a request to stop, the frameworks document's "You want to stop here. Would you like to continue chatting?" is no longer used (it leaves `CLIENT_LINES`) until Lolly says otherwise. The conclusion only note of ADR-016 is replaced by this, and `backend/content/prompts/somatic.md`'s check in rules ("must not summarize", "ask no question", the conclusion "left out of most") are rewritten to the bridge and reseeded.
- **AC-7** (Mani answers what they report): on the turn answering `somatic_practice` (the turn that retires the framework today, `orchestrator.send` near line 364), `[ctx]` carries `answering_practice: yes` with the framework id and style, and the prompt holds the rules for it. Mani responds in its own words to what they report: better or mixed names what shifted, in their words; unchanged or worse says so plainly and never says it worked; "it comes back" keeps the client's waves reply. Then **Chat More** and **Go to Library**. The body route (place buttons and the client's practices per place and style) is unchanged.
- **AC-8** (the outcome is stored): on that turn, when their reply says how they feel, the model reports `felt_after` (`better`, `mixed`, `unchanged`, `worse`, `unsure`) as a top level reply field, and one `public.framework_outcomes` row is written in the same transaction as the turn, with the framework, style, ending and body place. `felt_after` and `ending` are plain strings in the schema, normalised in `repairs`, and an unknown value is dropped before any insert, so a stray value never aborts the turn. No row when no practice was given (they declined the somatic check, or the pain safety reply was sent), when they leave or change the subject, when a safety concern (screen or pausing model flag) is raised on that turn, or when `create_message_pair` reports a duplicate. The insert is `on conflict do nothing`. When the practice was answered and no `felt_after` came back, a note is logged. A person can read only their own rows; only `mani_service` inserts; nobody updates; rows go when the account or the thread goes.
- **AC-9** (no code enforces tone, a turn is one chat call): `backend/mani/chat/redraft.py` and the second model call in `orchestrator.send` are removed, so every chat turn makes exactly one call with purpose `chat` (ADR-002; the exercise pick on the retire turn and the client's schema retry are separate and unchanged). `redraft.offered` and `redraft.ruled_out` move to `repairs`. Repairs that police tone are removed: the feeling word log note, the stock phrase list, the name repair, the repeated clarification repair, and the button label word, feeling and length checks (labels at offers, the body route and endings are fixed by code). Structural repairs stay: script metadata stripping, button sanity (empty, duplicate, the one just tapped, at most three), unknown ids, no technique button while a framework runs, offer composition, the somatic route, the library section default.
- **AC-10** (the call log keeps the turn's decisions): `admin.llm_calls.facts` is replaced by `decision` (jsonb) on every chat call: `{"offered": <framework id or null>, "refused": <first_message | safety_concern | vetoed | cooling_down | finished | running | unknown | null>, "step_from": <step or null>, "step_to": <step or null>, "ending": <ending or null>, "felt_after": <outcome or null>}`, ids and codes only, never words, so a wrong skip or a missing outcome can be traced at the checkpoint. `scripts/eval_replies.py` reads this line instead of the `facts` and `redrafting` log lines.
- **AC-11** (evals measure the client's rules, never block): `tests/evals/validators.py` and `scripts/client_style_counts.py` drop the checks for removed rules (question over 15 words, flagged words, size words including "a lot", "sounds like" as a stock phrase, ranking, either/or after stuck) and keep feeling words, labelling, clinical terms, multiple questions, dashes, lead clauses, repeated phrases and openers, assumed motive, forced positivity, the framework name, and the ending buttons. Tests that assert removed behaviour are deleted; the whole `pytest` passes with `filterwarnings = error` and integration tests not skipped; `scripts/test_db.sh --local` passes with the new table's assertions.
- **AC-12** (the records follow): ADR-006, 007, 008, 011, 012, 013, 014, 015, 016 and 017 are deleted with their lines in `mani-vault/Decisions/_Index.md`; specs 0002 to 0009 are deleted; a new ADR-019 records the source order and Lolly's principles; Lolly's email is transcribed into `backend/docs/specs/`; `backend/PORT-STATUS.md`, `.claude/BACKEND.md` and `backend/docs/database-schema-reference.md` describe the result.
- **AC-13** (safety is untouched): `backend/mani/chat/safety.py`, the crisis replies, the model flag kinds and their pause, the grief veto, RLS and token checks behave as before, and their tests pass unchanged.
- **AC-14** (the checkpoint): muhammad holds one fresh conversation in each style in chat-tester on the real model, end to end through the offer, the steps, the somatic check, the response after it and the close, and reads them, with Lolly, against her nine points: naturalness, consistency across the styles, listening versus interrogating, framework choice and introduction, recognising a completed step, pivoting when the person changes direction, the move into the somatic check, the response after it, and the close. What they find is written in a journal note, and the outcome rows of those conversations are read with SQL.

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Option 2: replace the conversation rules in place, in one branch, keeping the structural and safety guards.

The client's documents become the only source of conversation rules: the model judges the offer, the steps and the synthesis, the code keeps six offer refusals, one extra attempt per step, the mandatory somatic check and the safety guards, and an effectiveness outcome is stored per framework ending.

## Feature design

**Data model sketch**:

`public.framework_outcomes` (new, one row per framework ending that got an answer):

| Column               | Type        | Rule                                                                                               |
| -------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `id`                 | uuid        | primary key, `gen_random_uuid()`                                                                   |
| `user_id`            | uuid        | not null, FK `auth.users` on delete cascade, indexed                                               |
| `thread_id`          | uuid        | not null, FK `public.threads` on delete cascade, indexed                                           |
| `message_id`         | uuid        | not null, **unique**, FK `public.messages` on delete cascade (the person's reply that reported it) |
| `framework_id`       | text        | not null, FK `admin.frameworks` on delete restrict, indexed                                        |
| `conversation_style` | text        | not null, check in (`direct`, `supportive`, `reflective`)                                          |
| `ending`             | text        | not null, check in (`resolved`, `pivoted`, `stopped`)                                              |
| `body_place`         | text        | null, check in (`chest`, `head`, `stomach`, `elsewhere`)                                           |
| `outcome`            | text        | not null, check in (`better`, `mixed`, `unchanged`, `worse`, `unsure`)                             |
| `created_at`         | timestamptz | not null, default `now()`                                                                          |

A second unique key, (`thread_id`, `framework_id`, `ending`), stops two concurrent retries of the same retire turn without a client message id from writing twice (a finished framework is never offered again in the thread, so a real second row for that key cannot occur).

`public.thread_technique_state` gains `ending text null check in (resolved, pivoted, stopped)`: set on the turn a framework moves to `somatic_checkin`, carried through every later write of the row (`threads.apply` keeps it in its conflict update), cleared when the row is written for a new offer. Its `known` column stays, used only for `{"stuck": true}`.

`admin.llm_calls`: migration 013 (written today, not committed) is rewritten so the column is `decision jsonb` instead of `facts jsonb`. Locally, 013 was applied by hand: drop `facts` and apply the new file.

Text with check constraints follows `threads.conversation_style` and `holds`, rather than new enum types.

**State transitions** (a framework run, `thread_technique_state`):

offered → declined (cooldown) | accepted → step … step (forward only, one extra attempt per step) → [resolved | pivoted | stopped, `ending` set] → `somatic_checkin` → `somatic_practice` (body route) → their answer, the retire turn (`framework_outcomes` row when it says how they feel) → **Chat More** / **Go to Library** → retired. A safety concern at any point pauses it, as today.

**API surface**: no new endpoint. Changes inside the existing chat turn (`POST /v1/threads/{thread_id}/messages`, `send`):

| What                                      | Change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Model reply schema (`mani/llm/schema.py`) | `facts` removed; top level `ending` (string: `resolved`, `pivoted`, `stopped`, or null) and `felt_after` (string: `better`, `mixed`, `unchanged`, `worse`, `unsure`, or null), normalised in `repairs`; `TechniqueState.step` no longer accepts the `answered` sentinel |
| Offer buttons in the turn response        | **Yes, let's try it** (technique), **Tell me more**, **I want to keep talking** (decline), set by code, replacing **Try it** and **Keep chatting**; the tap matching in `orchestrator` follows the new labels                                                           |
| Outcome reads                             | none in this spec; the team reads with SQL on the admin connection                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

**Key invariants**:

- One call with purpose `chat` per turn; nothing asks for a reply again.
- An offer is never shown on their first message (urgent excepted), under a safety concern, when vetoed, while a decline cools down, after a framework was finished in the thread, or while one runs.
- Offer and explanation buttons are set by code, never by the model.
- A framework step never moves backward; each step takes at most one counted extra attempt.
- Every accepted framework that ends without a safety pause reaches `somatic_checkin`, and its `ending` is never lost before retirement.
- At most one outcome row per person message and per (thread, framework, ending); no row is ever updated.
- No quoted words of the person in `admin.llm_calls`.

**Security model**: `framework_outcomes` is health data (how a person felt after a mental health exercise). RLS is enabled in the creating migration, with a policy per operation: select where `(select auth.uid()) = user_id`; insert where the same holds and the thread belongs to them (as `thread_technique_state`'s insert policy does); no update policy; delete only through account deletion, which needs both the `auth_admin_cascade_delete` policy and `grant delete on public.framework_outcomes to supabase_auth_admin`, as migration 005 does for the other tables. Grants: `authenticated` gets SELECT; INSERT goes to `mani_service` only, never `authenticated`, so a person cannot forge an outcome through PostgREST. `mani_service` is never granted to `authenticator`. `tests/sql/test_grants.sql`, `test_rls.sql` and `tests/integration/test_account_deletion_cascade.py` cover the new table. The table is in `public` but `auto_expose_new_tables` is false, so the grants are the whole story.

**Configuration required**: none.

**Critical test scenarios**:

- Happy path: a scripted conversation reaches an offer chosen by the model, shows three buttons, **Tell me more** returns the explanation with two buttons, the person accepts, the model skips a step already answered, ends the framework, the check in line follows Mani's bridge, the person says "a bit better but still tight" after the practice, and one outcome row with `mixed` is stored, verifies **AC-3**, **AC-4**, **AC-5**, **AC-6**, **AC-8**.
- Refusals: an offer on the first message (and one after an untapped style), under a screen concern, after "my dog died" for Behavioral Activation, one turn after a decline, and after a finished framework each lose their buttons with one `chat` call, and the call log records the reason; "about to send it" on the first message still gets DBT STOP, verifies **AC-3**, **AC-9**, **AC-10**.
- Steps: a hold is a step equal to the stored one; a second hold on it is recorded as a move; a backward step is refused; `ending: stopped` on any step records `somatic_checkin`; `ending` survives the move to `somatic_practice`, verifies **AC-5**, **AC-6**.
- Outcome failures: a retried turn and a duplicate message write no second row; a declined somatic check, the pain reply, a safety concern on the retire turn, and an unknown `felt_after` value write none and the turn still succeeds, verifies **AC-8**.
- Permission: as an unprivileged `authenticated` user, inserting or updating an outcome row is refused and another person's rows are invisible; deleting the account removes the rows, verifies **AC-8**.
- Safety: the crisis and concern tests pass unchanged and none of them depends on `facts`, verifies **AC-13**.
