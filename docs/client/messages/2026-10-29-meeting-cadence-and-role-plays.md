## The points

- **The cadence** (Lolly): acknowledge, open the window, insight, ask a question. Opening the window widens from
  "you" to the situation and the challenge in it, not only the feeling.
- **Never show the notes, never summarise** (Lolly). The person knows what they said; MANI gives what it makes of it.
  Progress lives in the insight.
- **The internal barometer** (Lolly): when she knows the core, she gives the insight and asks whether they want to
  work through it with a framework, with a reason it fits. No summary check.
- **"I don't know"**: ask "Is what's bothering you the situation, or something more about yourself?"
- **Two emotions**: ask which is more pervasive, or whether it is both.
- **Stay on the core**: when they bring in another situation, name it and bring them back to the one thing.
- **Action**: "I didn't fix, I suggested." Suggestions about what is in their control, with the words to use, theirs
  to choose.
- **Directive**: less about the feeling, more about the challenge and what they can control.
- **Somatic** (Michaela): later, not part of this round.
