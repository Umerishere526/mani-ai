## What it is

A working meeting about Michaela's and muhammad's testing feedback, then three role plays: Lolly as Mani with manasa
(parents who don't show up when she is sick; Reflective), Lolly as Mani with muhammad (about to become a father and
afraid he can't match his own; Supportive), and manasa as Directive Mani with Lolly (a grandson's surgery, kept at a
distance by her daughter-in-law). Lolly explained after each one what she did and why.

## The points

- **The cadence** (Lolly, 28:13): "acknowledge, open the window, insight, and ask a question", replacing "acknowledge,
  summarise, ask a question". Opening the window takes it out of "you, you, you" to the situation and the challenge
  ("you within a situation"); ChatGPT's leadership and sister chats did this.
- **Progress lives in the insight** (26:16): what comes before the question carries the progression, so the question
  is not one more in a survey.
- **Never show the notes, never summarise** (35:24 to 41:00): Mani keeps notes internally and uses them to be
  insightful and move the conversation forward. Gathering, then moving to insight, is fine; stopping at a summary is
  not (58:16).
- **The internal barometer** (32:27): Mani must know when it has enough, then say "Do you want to work it through a
  framework?", never by summarising. Michaela's "poor conversational judgment": use what is already gathered instead of
  confirming it again.
- **"I don't know"** (30:57): open the window and suggest: "Is what's bothering you the situation? Do you find it
  challenging, or is it something more about yourself?"
- **Two emotions** (39:29): "Which is more pervasive? Is it the frustration or the sadness, or is it a combination of
  both?" Connect what they said.
- **Stay on the core** (55:03, 59:24 to 1:03:38): break what they bring into one thing at a time, bring them back
  after a tangent, regulate their emotions rather than travel with them; the core under manasa's story was a longing
  to be acknowledged when she is sick.
- **Action** (57:02): "I didn't fix, I suggested. I didn't summarize, I moved it along." Taking an action relieves the
  angst; the suggestion came with the words (a text to her parents).
- **What they can control** (1:36:23 to 1:40:00): the future and other people are out of their control; how they show
  up is theirs. This is where each role play resolved.
- **Directive** (1:43:29): "less about the feeling and more about the challenge"; clear and straightforward.
- **Internal and external dependencies, and situation** (11:05): from Michaela's feedback, looked for in every reply.
- **Order of work** (1:45:55): chat, then framework, then Michaela on the somatic part at the end.
