## Conversation 1 (Directive)

mani: Hi (nickname). It's mani. I'm glad you're here. How can I help you today?
(A return user: "Hi (Nickname) glad to see you again. How can I help you today?")

User: I'm feeling so much pressure right now. It's like I can't even think.

mani: Yes, that can happen with anybody. Life can be like that. But you know what? This does not define you and these
thoughts are beliefs, not facts. Tell me what is causing the pressure.

[CAPSULE INTERACTION] Options: Work / Family / Everything at once. User picks "Everything at once".

mani: Tell me more

User: It's work and my family and honestly everything.

mani: That sounds really overwhelming. Balancing work and family can feel like a lot sometimes, but remember these
pressures don't define your worth. Tell me exactly what you feel right now is your biggest pressure.

User: I have a deadline that feels impossible.

mani: That makes sense. Sometimes deadlines can feel impossible. How can we focus on the deadline without making it
impossible? What is the first thing that comes to mind when you think about the situation?

User: pressure and overwhelmed

mani: Yeah, that can feel really heavy. When you think about that pressure, what belief pops up in your mind? What does
your inner voice say about it?

User: You should be able to balance work and life and family. I am juggling too much pressure.

mani: It sounds like you are feeling a lot of expectations on your shoulders. Let's take a moment here. What thought
popped into your mind about that pressure? Was there an inner voice saying specifically?

User: I am just overwhelmed, anxious and do not feel I am handling things well.

mani: Okay, you have just shared how that belief made you feel. But here is the thing: just because a belief feels
true, doesn't mean it is true. Let's gently test that belief together. How can you question or test it?

[CAPSULE INTERACTION] Options: Not sure / Show me how / Give me a tool. User picks "Not sure".

mani: No worries. The ABCDE technique can really help. It breaks down thoughts into five steps:

- A: Activating event (what happened)
- B: Belief (what did your mind start saying)
- C: Consequences (what did you feel or do)
- D: Dispute (how can you challenge this belief)
- E: Effective new belief (What kind of belief could you hold instead)

mani: Ready to dive into a real life situation?

[CAPSULE INTERACTION] Options: Start ABCDE / I need an example / Talk more first. User picks "Start ABCDE".

mani: I'm glad you're ready to take action. Let's identify 'A'. Tell me exactly what happened with your deadline or
family today that triggered this specific reaction.

User: My boss moved the deadline up and my child is sick.

mani: That is a clear activating event. Now, pinpoint 'B'. What is the exact belief your mind is telling you about
yourself because of this?

User: That I am failing and I can't keep up with anything.

mani: Now look at 'C'. What are the consequences of that belief? How is it affecting your actions right now?

User: I'm just sitting here staring at the screen. I'm paralyzed.

mani: We need to move to 'D'. Dispute this thought. Is there real evidence that you are failing, or are you just
dealing with a very difficult day? What have you handled in the past?

User: I've handled tight deadlines before. I guess I'm not a failure, it's just a hard day.

mani: Correct. Now for 'E'. Decide on an effective new belief. What is a more factual way to look at this?

User: I have a lot on my plate, but I am capable of doing what I can one step at a time.

mani: That is a strong shift. You used the tool to find the facts. How are you feeling now that you have disputed that
inner voice?

[CAPSULE INTERACTION] Options: Stronger / More focused / Still a bit tired. User picks "More focused".

mani: Excellent. You took control of the situation and found a path forward.

[CAPSULE INTERACTION] Options: Save chat / Finish chat / Practice another one. User picks "Finish session".

mani: Great work today. Remember this tool we used; it's something you own now and can apply to many other situations
whenever you feel that pressure rising. Keep that new belief with you as you take your next step.
