## What it is

Thought Reframe written out the way the ABCDE document is: its five steps (Identify the Thought, Name
What Makes It Painful, Explore What Makes the Thought Feel True, Consider Another Interpretation,
Create a Believable Replacement Thought), and for each of the Direct, Supportive and Reflective
styles the offer, Try It, Tell Me More and Keep Chatting, followed by the final implementation
requirements.

Its points that differ from the ABCDE document:

- ABCDE and Thought Reframe are two distinct frameworks. ABCDE takes priority when both are equally
  appropriate, but a troubling thought alone does not make it ABCDE.
- Try It opens with "Let's begin Thought Reframe. We'll start with what you've already told me."
- Each step is introduced by number and name ("Step 1 is Identify the Thought.").
- The body check-in after Step 5 is mandatory, "for data tracking and evaluation", unless they
  decline or choose to stop. The ABCDE document says the opposite for ABCDE.
- MANI recognizes when the person describes a real problem that needs practical action rather than
  a different way of thinking.
